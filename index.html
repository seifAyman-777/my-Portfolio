The Methodology

How Great Copy Gets Results: The 4-Step Framework

No guessing, no fluff. Every word is backed by intense customer research and proven direct-response mechanics.

01

Customer & Review Mining

I analyze customer reviews, reddit threads, and competitor feedback to pull the exact natural language, frustrations, and desires your buyers use.

02

Angle & Hook Engineering

We construct 3-5 unique messaging angles (e.g. speed, convenience, status, problem-avoidance) to test which trigger yields the highest response.

03

Psychological Draft Build

Drafting copy structured around proven frameworks (PAS, AIDA, Rule of One) ensuring total clarity, smooth transitions, and relentless momentum.

04

Friction-Free CTA Optimization

Removing anxiety around price, commitment, and effort. Replacing generic CTAs with high-value action triggers that boost conversion.
